# Marcus T. (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)

## Profile
Biomedical engineer who owns the maintenance and configuration of a large infusion pump fleet.

## Claims
- [E13] constraint: Responsible for roughly four hundred pumps; needs an advance validation window for firmware changes rather than short notice.
- [E14, E18] behavior: Quickly sorts nurse calls into training problems versus device problems, and reads nurse workarounds as signs the design failed.
- [E15] behavior: Reads the service manual before marketing material and treats a thin manual as a quality signal.
- [E16] goal: Wants EHR interoperability without a custom interface he has to maintain.
- [E17] constraint: Implements drug library content approved by the medication safety committee and does not decide that content.
- [E5] context: Is the person nurses call first when a pump misbehaves, ahead of the vendor hotline.
