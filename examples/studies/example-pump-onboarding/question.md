# Research question (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE STUDY)

## Decision this informs
Whether the onboarding for a new infusion pump should lead with bedside job aids or with a revised in-service.

## Question
Where in device onboarding do clinicians and biomed staff work around the intended steps, and which questions in our discussion guide surface that reliably?

## What would change our mind
Evidence that nurses actually do read and follow the on-screen prompts and the quick-reference, which would move effort back to the in-service.

## What this study cannot tell us
How common any workaround is, or workarounds neither persona's evidence contains.
